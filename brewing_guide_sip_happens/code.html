Brewing Guide

Unlock the full potential of your beans with our curated guide to the perfect artisan cup.

Step 01

Grind the Beans

Choose a medium-fine grind size, similar to sea salt. Freshness is paramount—grind only what you intend to brew immediately to preserve the delicate aromatic oils.

BURR GRINDER 20G BEANS
Step 02

Heat the Water

Target a temperature between 195°F and 205°F (90°C-96°C). Boiling water will scorch the grounds, while cooler water won't extract the full complexity of the roast.

GOOSENECK KETTLE 93° CELSIUS
Step 03

Brew Perfectly

Begin with a 30-second bloom, pouring just enough water to saturate the grounds. Follow with slow, concentric circles, maintaining a steady flow for a total brew time of 3:30 minutes.

THE BLOOM 3:30 TIMER
Step 04

Pour into Mug

Swirl the carafe gently to aerate and equalize the brew's temperature. Pour into a pre-warmed ceramic vessel to maintain the thermal profile and enhance the tactile experience.

PRE-WARMED CERAMIC MUG
Step 05

Enjoy the Aroma

Pause. Before your first sip, inhale the complex bouquet of notes—from floral jasmine to deep, toasted caramel. Savor the moment; you've crafted excellence.

AROMATIC NOTES SLOW LIVING
