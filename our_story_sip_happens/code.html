The Slow-Living Philosophy

In an age of instant gratification, Sip Happens was born from the desire to pause. We curated a space where the hiss of the steam wand and the aroma of freshly ground single-origin beans dictate the pace of the afternoon.

Every roast profile we develop is a months-long conversation between our master roasters and the farmers who nurture the soil. We don't just source coffee; we honor the lineage of every cherry.

Matthias Thorne, Founder
